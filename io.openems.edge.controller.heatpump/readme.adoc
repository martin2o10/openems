= Heat Pump Controllers

Controls heat pumps that implement the `ManagedHeatPump` nature. The structure follows the EVSE Controllers:

- `Controller.HeatPump.Single` — one Controller per heat pump: Mode, anti-cycling protection and Energy Scheduler.
- `Controller.HeatPump.Cluster` — distributes power to all single Controllers.

The Controllers never override the own control of the heat pump; they only add consumption (or lock the heat pump). A `ManagedHeatPump` in read-only mode is not controlled, but all decisions are visible in the Channels of the Controllers.

== Controller.HeatPump.Single

.Modes
* `OWN_CONTROL`: no intervention (default)
* `SURPLUS`: use surplus power, e.g. from PV
* `FORCE`: heat with maximum power (up to the maximum storage temperature of the heat pump)
* `SMART`: the Energy Scheduler chooses per period between `SURPLUS` and `FORCE`, e.g. to pre-heat in cheap periods of a dynamic tariff

.Anti-cycling protection (`AntiCycling`)
* A requested power is only applied after a start delay of 60 seconds (except for `FORCE`) and after the minimum off time of the compressor
* Once applied, the power is held at least at minimum power for the minimum run time
* A lock is always applied immediately

.Energy Scheduler (`EnergyScheduler`)
The heat storage is simulated with a simple model: storage volume between minimum and maximum temperature, a constant average heat demand and an estimated COP. If the storage is empty, the own control of the heat pump covers the demand.

== Controller.HeatPump.Cluster

The cluster runs in every Cycle:

1. Base action per Mode: `OWN_CONTROL`, or maximum power for `FORCE`
2. `SurplusDistribution`: surplus = power of heat pumps in `SURPLUS` mode - grid buy - storage discharge (like the EVSE Cluster); distributed in the configured order of the Controllers
3. `GridLimit` (optional): if the grid buy power exceeds the configured maximum, controller-increased power is reduced in reverse order. The own control of the heat pumps is never restricted.

Each goal is implemented in its own class without dependencies on the others, so it can be removed or replaced separately.

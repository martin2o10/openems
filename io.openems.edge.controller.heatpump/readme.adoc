= Heat Pump Controllers

Controls heat pumps that implement the `ManagedHeatPump` nature, following the structure of the EVSE Controllers:

- `Controller.HeatPump.Single`: one Controller per heat pump with Mode (`OWN_CONTROL`, `SURPLUS`, `FORCE`, `SMART`), anti-cycling protection and Energy Scheduler
- `Controller.HeatPump.Cluster`: distributes surplus power to all single Controllers in the configured order

The Controllers never override the own control of the heat pump; they only request additional consumption. Heat pumps in read-only mode are not controlled, but all decisions are visible in the Channels.

In `SMART` mode the Energy Scheduler chooses per period between `SURPLUS` and `FORCE`, based on a simple storage model (volume between minimum and maximum temperature, constant average heat demand, estimated COP).
